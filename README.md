# FiberBoard

Turn a photo of your splice whiteboard into a filled Spectrum-style cut sheet — Excel, PDF, and Google Earth KMZ. No retyping at the truck.

Photograph the board the way you already write it. Get the official cut sheet back.

## The problem

Splice crews write everything on a whiteboard at the enclosure — sheath, buffer colors, fiber counts, MST taps, butt splices, coordinates. Then someone has to retype all of it into a cut sheet at the truck. That's where errors and lost time happen.

## What it does

1. Snap a photo of the whiteboard
2. OCR + template matching fills the cut sheet automatically
3. Export to Excel, PDF, or Google Earth KMZ
4. Conflict flags catch mismatches before you leave the site

## Who it's for

OSP / FTTH field techs, splicing crews, and contractors running GPON drops for Spectrum and similar providers.
